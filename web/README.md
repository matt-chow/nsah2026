# Frontend experience

Discover and Explore share the selected field, observation, reference, and candidate.
Discover adds one observation task per chapter. On narrow screens its guidance stays in
page flow; Explore and Analyze keep the collapsible evidence sheet. Candidate selection
pauses playback, shows a flagged date, and centres the selected spot. Changing a story
step stops timeline playback before applying the chapter's view.

A/B playback alternates two images; timeline playback visits the prepared comparable dates.
The combined typical sky is labelled separately from dated observations. Markers are
unconfirmed leads; their shape and line style are explained beside the image. Light-curve
values also have a keyboard-accessible date list. Illustrations are labelled separately.

All measurements, compatibility decisions, calibration, and provenance remain pipeline-owned.

Verify with `npm run check`, then test Discover → Explore → select a lead at desktop,
360px, and 390px widths, including keyboard-only and reduced-motion use. Check the existing
simulated fixture's refused comparison and the real fields; simulated data must stay labelled.
