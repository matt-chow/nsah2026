SPHEREx Sky Flipbook

Pick a patch of sky. Every SPHEREx exposure of it in one detector band plays overhead in time order, over the static all-sky atlas. Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom; red marks = target.

The 3D landscape needs WebGL, which this browser can't provide. Showing the sky flat instead (target at the centre, north up, east left); zoom and playback still work.

Backdrop: the SPHEREx all-sky atlas (a coadd of every DR1 exposure, so it never changes). Centre square: the individual exposures, in time order. Both are display stretches, not measurements.
