SPHEREx Sky Flipbook

Pick a patch of sky. Every SPHEREx image of it is aligned and painted overhead, in time order. Drag to look around; the target is marked in red in the southern sky.

Backdrop: the SPHEREx all-sky atlas (a coadd of every DR1 exposure, so it does not change with time). Centre square: the individual exposures, in time order. Both are display stretches, not measurements.
